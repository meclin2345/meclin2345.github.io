About this list

This page lists the Best Paper awards and named special awards (theme, resource/dataset, social impact, low-resource and similar) from the main conference of each edition below. Outstanding Paper awards, honourable mentions, demo, student research workshop and area chair awards are not included.

Covered: ACL 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025 · EMNLP 2023, 2024, 2025 · NAACL 2022, 2025 · EACL 2023, 2024 · COLING 2025. Not yet covered: other editions (for example NAACL 2024, EMNLP 2022, COLING 2022, LREC-COLING 2024, AACL and CoNLL), because I couldn’t check their full official winner lists.

How entries are checked: each paper’s award comes from the conference’s official award announcement (linked as “Award source” on every card). Its title, authors, abstract and link come from the paper’s ACL Anthology record. Last checked: 21 November 2026.

The list is maintained in the open. If you spot a missing or incorrect entry, or want to share your reading notes, open an issue or pull request on GitHub. Please include a link to the official award announcement for any paper you add.

This page is inspired by the belief that the best research should be readable by everyone — not locked behind paywalls and jargon. If a paper reshaped how you think about language technology, especially for low-resource or multilingual contexts, your note could be the thing that helps the next person have the same breakthrough.

🤝 Add your reading notes

Each paper card has an expandable notes section. Write what you learned, what surprised you, or how it connects to your work. Notes are saved only in this browser. To share one, copy it and open an issue or pull request on GitHub.
