Research

I study how language technology serves, misinforms and represents people who speak low-resource languages, starting with Tamil. Each dataset I build begins where experts have already made a judgement: professional fact-checkers, physicians, a public examination board and the courts. I take it through sourcing, annotation, quality control and expert validation, generate synthetic data where real data is scarce, and then use it to test how AI behaves across languages.

  • Misinformation on social media. Text, image and speech datasets built from Tamil fact-checks, and an explainable verifier that grounds its Tamil explanations in retrieved evidence. This is the focus of my Ph.D.
  • Health misinformation. Severity grading, a new claim-verification task, for India-specific health claims checked by physician-reviewed fact-checkers.
  • LLMs across languages. Paired English–Tamil and English–Hindi–Tamil audits of what LLMs know, and which political stances they take, depending on the language of the prompt.
  • AI and institutions. A bilingual corpus of district-court records that tests whether LLM-written summaries can be trusted as research data.

Datasets and audits

TamilFacts-X

Published

17,271 Tamil social media claims (2017–2026) verified by three IFCN-signatory fact-checking organizations, each with an image or image description, six synthetic speech renderings across 12 speakers and four registers, and fact-checker-written Tamil reasoning.

Speaker and style are assigned by hashing, so the synthetic audio carries no label signal. Two professional fact-checkers rated 100 reasoning traces (weighted κ 0.74–0.88).

Size
17,271 claims
Modalities
Text, image, speech (103,626 audio files)
Language
Tamil, including colloquial and code-mixed
Paper
J2

MedFactIndia

Released, CC-BY 4.0

2,745 health claims circulating on Indian social media, verified by physician-reviewed fact-checkers and paired with expert medical explanations. It introduces severity grading as a new claim-verification task.

171 raw verdict labels were normalized onto an ordered three-level scale (κ 0.70 blind, 0.81 with evidence). Models struggle most with intermediate-severity claims, often labelling them False.

Size
2,745 claims
Language
English (Indian sources)
Task
Health misinformation severity grading
Paper
C2

TamilFacts

Published

The first text–image–speech dataset of fact-checked Tamil social media content, drawn from four Tamil fact-checking sites, with 13 attributes per article and an 884-minute synthetic speech corpus.

Benchmarks cover 96 text–image–speech embedding combinations, with image forensics, speech denoising and cost-sensitive learning for class imbalance.

Size
7,934 articles
Modalities
Text, image, speech
Language
Tamil
Papers
C1, J1

TamilCourt-CBE

Under review

31,952 district-court records from Coimbatore (2012–2025), in their original Tamil and English, turned into 50+ structured fields by an LLM and graded by a nine-stage quality-control pipeline.

On a 100-case gold set, two legal annotators confirmed 94–100% LLM label accuracy. Sensitive cases are pseudonymized and access is gated.

Size
31,952 records
Languages
Tamil, English
Tasks
Judgment prediction, bail prediction, domain classification
Paper
U1

TamilBench

Under review

7,840 English–Tamil question pairs published bilingually by the Tamil Nadu Public Service Commission, each pair sharing one official answer key, spanning Tamil Nadu history, culture, literature, governance and aptitude.

A chance-corrected retention score and an option-collapse check show that two Tamil-adapted 7B models score near the majority baseline.

Size
7,840 question pairs
Languages
English, Tamil (parallel)
Task
Cross-lingual LLM evaluation
Paper
U2

Indic Political Compass audit

Under review

40 India-specific policy propositions with native-speaker translations, used to measure the political stances of five LLMs and masked language models.

Stances that lean libertarian in English move toward the centre in Tamil. Tested with two prompt templates, calibrated log-likelihood scoring and exact permutation tests.

Size
40 propositions in 3 languages
Languages
English, Hindi, Tamil
Task
Cross-lingual political bias audit
Paper
U3
